<p>A study of 1,997 participants playing a minority game with bots found that 30% followed the majority, despite theoretical expectations of no herding. The study explored how humans interact with automated entities and how their behavior changes when they are aware of interacting with bots. Herding behavior persisted even when participants were aware of interacting with automated entities.</p>

<pre>
Herding can be rational when the group has better information than the individual (Couzin et al., 2005).
